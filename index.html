Asher Cantrell

Asher Cantrell is the author of Other Gods, an anthology of short horror, and The Tribulations of Paul, a cosmic horror novella.

He has worked in tech and digital media as an author, editor, publisher, and engineer. In his spare time, he reads and watches a lot of horror, plays 30-year-old video games, and consumes an alarming amount of coffee.
